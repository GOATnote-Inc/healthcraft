# Next actionable user-value gap

The highest-value next increment is an independently assessable **operator incident report**, followed by a small feasibility session with actual intended users comparing assisted evidence navigation with raw-trace review. Additional model runs on these eight development cases do not establish that a person can reach a correct conclusion faster.

Current support is real but incomplete: `docs/RECONCILIATION_CASEBOOK.md:108` explicitly says the v1 tutorial is not a v2 adjudicator and lacks identified patient/encounter and incident fields; `docs/OPERATOR_REVIEW.md:80` validates answers/citations, while `:97` permits only uncollected or self-reported time. `docs/RECONCILIATION_LOCAL_MODELS.md:91` keeps independent label review, operator timing and valid-report adjudication pending. The release plan's primary proposed endpoint is time to a **valid** report, with invalid/missing trials charged their predeclared cap (`docs/RELEASE_EVIDENCE_PLAN.md:28`). Neither response-format validity nor a model's mechanical verdict measures that endpoint.

## Small implementation-ready step

Create a separately versioned v2 incident-report contract, leaving v1 tutorials immutable. Require the operator to identify the requested target and any observed wrong target; describe the incident or explicitly record no incident/unassessed; identify implicated source IDs and action/readback indices with exact evidence references; and distinguish acknowledged write, actual new storage, actual retrieval of stored text and source/content correctness. Preserve missing/unassessed/abstained rows and all assigned opportunities. Do not fill these answers from the oracle.

Pair this with a coordinator-only report-validity adjudication form: assess whether the submitted attribution and incident are supported, whether material errors/unknowns are omitted, and whether citations actually substantiate the report. Record independent reviewers' decisions and disagreements without automated majority truth labels. Unit tests can establish the schema, immutable imports, source binding and denominator behavior; they cannot establish report validity in use. The author-shared engineering expectations remain provisional inputs, not independent clinical labels.

## Then acquire the missing evidence

Use the existing exposed cases only to test workflow feasibility with actual clinical-AI evaluators/researchers; do not rebrand them as held out. Present the same public task specification and captured source/action records in both raw and assisted arms, with identical navigation/data access except the declared diagnostic assistance. Prespecify which source ledger information is intended operator input; keep adjudicator answer labels out of both arms. Reusing a clinical review protocol would confuse a mechanical incident-review task with clinical judgment.

Before any claim-bearing study, independently review the report-validity rubric, author/review fresh scenario families, register assignments/order and analysis, define capped complete-denominator timing, and obtain an independent registration receipt. Instrument elapsed and active time under a declared start/pause/submit policy; self-reported time is not an instrumented primary endpoint. Missing or invalid reports stay in the denominator at the cap. The proposed 0.80 time ratio and -0.02 valid-completion margin require reviewer/statistical ratification, not opportunistic use after outcomes. See `docs/RELEASE_EVIDENCE_PLAN.md:23-53`.

The immediately runnable comparison is **assisted review versus raw-trace review on the same synthetic workflow**. That can support a narrow incremental operator-utility claim if a future valid study succeeds. It does not establish superiority over Corecraft, Archangel Health, Baseten, HealthAgentBench or all available alternatives. Human participants, independent adjudication, clinical relevance review, study registration and held-out outcomes have not been supplied by this engineering work. `docs/RELEASE_EVIDENCE_PLAN.md:75-108` keeps those requirements before formal red team and publication. No outreach or participant labels were created in this review.
