# Care and imaging development evidence

These captures document ordinary engineering review and TDD, not clinical
calibration, formal red-team review, model performance or comparative value.
The care and imaging audits used frozen source based on local commit
`aa21717d0fa6454e4e7af51d3f50206ec2c6026b`. Their recorded source hashes and
reproducer files preserve the original temporary paths. That source snapshot
is reconstructible from the baseline commit; its duplicate tree is not included.

The care reproduction exercises actual retrieval, discharge and validation.
Its explicitly labeled representation-only control is not an observed clinical
administration or a new drug-interaction rule. The selector inventory reviews
22 direct fields, 32 supplied groups and 29 tasks. The imaging reproduction and
inventory distinguish source entries from performed studies and identify the
eight exact conditional guidance fields kept out of observations.

Failing logs capture defects before their respective repairs: invented
administrations, missing care, unavailable encounter validation, discovery
metadata, partial findings, mixed-key discharge rendering, imaging projection
and conditional visibility. Probe logs also preserve failures for native
response roles, identity binding and interrupted-request journaling. They are
not final-candidate failures. Each file's
copy hash and original location is recorded in `capture-manifest.json`.

The final contract is documented in `docs/CARE_IMAGING_FIDELITY.md`. Frozen
candidate validation and fresh local-model attempts are recorded separately;
they do not rewrite these baseline captures or any historical results.
